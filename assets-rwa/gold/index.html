Gold: ETFs, Digital Gold and Tokenised Gold

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Gold can be accessed through products that look similar on a price chart but work very differently underneath. Physical gold, Gold ETFs, digital gold and blockchain-based tokens such as PAXG or XAUT can differ in legal ownership, custody, fees, redemption, liquidity and counterparty risk. CoinRaahi explains those differences before comparing returns.

Physical gold

Physical gold gives direct possession of the metal, but comes with storage, authenticity, making charges, insurance and resale spreads. It provides a useful baseline for comparing other gold products.

Gold ETFs

A Gold ETF generally gives exposure through fund units traded in the securities market. Check the expense ratio, tracking difference, liquidity, demat or broker requirements, and fund custody. Gold ETFs are regulated investment products in India; see SEBI for the regulatory framework.

Digital gold

Digital gold products usually involve a provider arranging storage of physical gold on behalf of users. Important questions: who owns the gold, where is it stored, can it be redeemed, are there minimums, and what happens if the provider fails. “Digital” does not automatically mean “on-chain”.

Tokenised gold

Blockchain-based gold tokens such as PAXG and XAUT are designed to represent gold exposure through tokens. But users should still check the issuer, custodian, reserve verification, redemption terms, supported blockchain, wallet compatibility and jurisdiction.

“Backed by gold” is not enough

  1. Who legally owns the gold?
  2. Where is the gold stored?
  3. Who verifies the reserves?
  4. Can you redeem the product?
  5. What is the minimum redemption amount?
  6. What fees apply?
  7. Which jurisdiction governs the product?
  8. What happens if the provider becomes insolvent?

This is more useful than relying on the phrase “100% backed”.

Costs to compare

Depending on the product: making charges, GST, ETF expense ratio, brokerage, spread, custody, blockchain network fee, exchange fee and redemption charge. Total cost matters more than one advertised fee.

Liquidity and exit

Ask whether you can sell during market hours only, redeem for physical gold, transfer the token, whether there is a minimum, and where the deepest liquidity is. The best exit route depends on the product structure.

Frequently asked questions

Is digital gold the same as a Gold ETF?

No. They have different ownership, custody and market structures.

Is PAXG the same as physical gold?

No. PAXG is a tokenised product backed under an issuer and custody structure.

Is XAUT an ETF?

No. XAUT is a blockchain-based gold token, not a traditional ETF.

Which gold product is safest?

Each has different risks. Compare custody, issuer, liquidity and redemption rather than assuming one format is universally safer.

Can tokenised gold be redeemed?

Redemption depends on the issuer’s terms, eligibility and minimum requirements.
