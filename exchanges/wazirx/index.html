WazirX Guides for India

Last updated: 14 September 2026

WazirX is one of India’s best-known crypto exchanges, but its recent history is different from most other platforms. Before using it, you need to understand the 2024 incident, the recovery process, and what a new deposit means today.

The 2024 incident and recovery

In July 2024, a large amount of crypto — reported figures are around US$230–235 million — was drained from a WazirX multisig wallet that was managed under a third-party custody arrangement. The incident has been widely attributed to the North Korea–linked Lazarus Group by blockchain-analysis firms and investigators.

Trading and withdrawals were then frozen while a court-supervised restructuring was put in place under Singapore law. A scheme of arrangement was sanctioned in October 2025, and trading resumed later that month.

As part of the recovery, eligible users received an initial distribution of a majority of their pre-hack balances. A remaining portion — reported in the region of 15% — was represented by non-tradable Recovery Tokens, whose value depends on WazirX generating future income to fund buybacks. Those tokens cannot be freely sold or withdrawn.

What this means for you. If you held funds on WazirX before the 2024 incident, part of that value may still be represented by Recovery Tokens rather than an immediately available balance. Check the app’s fund details for your own allocation.

Is WazirX operating now?

WazirX resumed trading in late 2025 and is FIU-IND registered in India as of 2026. New deposits made after the restart are treated separately from the earlier recovery pool, which is an important distinction when you are deciding whether to use the platform.

FIU-IND registration relates to anti-money-laundering compliance. It should not be read as a government endorsement, a guarantee of safety, or an investment recommendation.

INR access and P2P

WazirX has historically been known for its P2P market as an INR on- and off-ramp, which some Indian users rely on when direct UPI or bank rails are unavailable or restricted. P2P lets buyers and sellers transact directly, which also means you are dealing with a counterparty rather than the exchange’s own order book.

Before funding, check the supported payment methods, the counterparty’s terms, and the current INR deposit and withdrawal options shown for your account.

Fees

WazirX’s fee structure has changed since the restart. It has offered a zero-fee promotion on selected pairs (including USDT/INR) under a “WazirX Zero” model, later adding a subscription option, and it launched INR-settled futures in May 2026 with maker/taker fees reported around 0.02%/0.04%.

As with any exchange, the headline trading fee is not the whole cost. Compare the spread, the INR withdrawal cost, the crypto withdrawal fee and any conversion cost for the specific route you intend to use. Related: Crypto Exchange Fees & Withdrawals.

Custody and withdrawals

After the 2024 incident, WazirX migrated its custody to BitGo Trust, with cold-storage multisig and a reported insurance cover of up to US$250 million. Custody still means the platform controls the assets you hold on it — which is different from self-custody, where you control the keys.

Crypto withdrawal availability can depend on the asset, network, account status and current policy. If you intend to move assets out, check the current withdrawal screen rather than relying on an older review.

Tax records

Useful platform records can include transaction history, buy/sell data, TDS information where relevant and downloadable statements. Keep your own copies, and record any Recovery Token allocation carefully — it may have tax implications that are best checked with a professional. See our crypto tax in India guide.

WazirX vs CoinDCX vs CoinSwitch vs Binance

Rather than ask “which is best?”, ask the questions that matter for your use case:

  • Which has the most convenient INR route for you?
  • Which allows the crypto withdrawals you need?
  • What is each platform’s recent security and custody record?
  • Which gives clearer tax and transaction records?
  • Which total cost is lower for your intended route?

What to check before using WazirX

  • Whether your funds (if any) are pre-hack or a new deposit.
  • Current INR deposit, withdrawal and P2P options.
  • Current trading fees, spread and withdrawal costs.
  • Whether the assets you want can be withdrawn to your own wallet.
  • That you have your own records for tax purposes.
