CoinDCX Guides for India

Last updated: 6 September 2026

CoinDCX is one of the better-known Indian crypto platforms, but choosing or using it involves more than checking whether INR deposits are available.

Users should also understand how KYC works, how trading costs are calculated, whether a particular crypto asset can be withdrawn, how tax records are provided and what happens when an account or transfer is delayed.

This page brings CoinRaahi’s CoinDCX guides together in one place.

Platform features and fees can change. Always check the current information shown in your account and official CoinDCX documentation before acting.

CoinDCX KYC and account verification

KYC requirements are part of using a regulated crypto platform in India.

Depending on the account and current workflow, users may be asked to provide identity details, PAN information, facial verification or bank-related information.

Common issues can include name mismatch, document mismatch, verification pending, failed facial verification, bank account mismatch and account restrictions.

If verification fails, do not repeatedly upload random documents. First identify which field or document is causing the mismatch.

Related: Crypto Exchange KYC in India.

INR deposits and withdrawals

For many Indian users, INR convenience is one of the main reasons to use a domestic exchange.

Before depositing, check the supported payment method, minimum and maximum amount, processing time, whether the payment account must match the KYC name, and the deposit status inside the app.

For withdrawals, check the linked bank account, withdrawal limit, processing time, platform notices and whether maintenance is affecting withdrawals.

Do not assume that a successful INR deposit means crypto withdrawals will work in exactly the same way. These are separate systems.

Trading fees, spread and total cost

A trading fee is only one part of the cost. Suppose a user deposits ₹10,000 and buys a crypto asset. The total cost can include the trading fee, spread, the difference between the quoted and executed price, the withdrawal fee and any blockchain-related cost if the asset is moved externally.

CoinRaahi recommends comparing total route cost, not only the advertised trading fee.

CoinRaahi cost framework

Total Cost = Entry Cost + Trading Cost + Spread + Withdrawal/Transfer Cost + Exit Cost

Can you withdraw crypto?

This should be checked before buying an asset specifically for self-custody.

Confirm whether withdrawals are currently enabled, whether that specific coin can be withdrawn, which networks are supported, the minimum withdrawal amount, the withdrawal fee and destination network compatibility.

A coin being available for trading does not automatically mean every withdrawal network is available.

Custody: who controls the crypto?

Crypto held inside CoinDCX is custodial. That means the user has an account balance, while the platform manages the underlying wallet infrastructure and private keys.

This can be convenient for beginners, but it is different from holding crypto in a self-custody wallet.

Related: Exchange vs self-custody wallet.

Tax records and TDS

Indian users should keep accurate records even if the platform provides reports. Useful records include trade date, asset, quantity, purchase value, sale value, fees, TDS information where applicable, transaction history and bank statements.

Platform-generated reports can help, but users remain responsible for ensuring their own tax records are complete.

Related: Crypto Tax in India.

CoinDCX vs other exchanges

CoinDCX may be relevant to users who prioritise domestic INR access, India-focused reporting and local account workflows.

Other platforms may differ in coin selection, crypto withdrawal flexibility, advanced trading tools, global liquidity and fee structure.

The right comparison depends on what you actually need the platform to do.

Frequently asked questions

Is CoinDCX an Indian crypto exchange?

CoinDCX is an India-focused crypto platform serving users in the Indian market. Users should still check current product availability and platform terms.

Does CoinDCX require KYC?

Identity verification is generally required before using core account features.

Can I deposit INR into CoinDCX?

CoinDCX has offered INR funding routes, but payment methods and limits can change. Check the current account interface.

Can I withdraw crypto from CoinDCX?

Withdrawal availability can depend on the asset, network and account status.

Is CoinDCX cheaper than Binance or CoinSwitch?

Not always. Compare spread, trading fees, withdrawal costs and INR convenience together.
