CoinSwitch Guides for India

Last updated: 6 September 2026

CoinSwitch is often presented as a simple way for Indian users to access crypto, but simplicity in the app does not remove the need to understand the product underneath.

Before using any exchange, it is worth checking what assets are available, how pricing works, whether crypto withdrawals are supported, how custody works and what records you receive for tax purposes. This hub collects CoinRaahi’s CoinSwitch guides and comparisons.

Is CoinSwitch just a beginner app?

A clean interface can make a platform easier to use, but that does not automatically mean it is the best fit for every user. The important questions are:

  • Can you fund the account in INR?
  • What does a buy order really cost?
  • Can the crypto be withdrawn externally?
  • Who controls the assets?
  • What tax and transaction records are available?
  • What limits apply?

CoinRaahi focuses on these practical details rather than labelling a platform “good for beginners” without qualification.

INR access

CoinSwitch is designed around the Indian market, so INR access is an important part of the user experience.

Before funding the account, check the supported payment methods, account name requirements, deposit limits, withdrawal limits and expected processing times.

If a bank transfer or UPI payment is delayed, do not repeat the payment until you first confirm whether the original transaction is pending.

Understanding the price you pay

A user may see a market price, a quoted buy price, a quoted sell price, a fee and a spread. The difference between buy and sell prices can matter significantly, especially on smaller orders. That is why CoinRaahi does not compare exchanges only by headline fee.

Example. If BTC appears at ₹X on a market tracker but the actual purchase quote is higher, the difference may reflect spread, pricing structure or execution conditions.

Always compare the final amount received, not just the displayed percentage fee.

Can CoinSwitch crypto be withdrawn?

This is one of the most important questions for users who want to move assets to an external wallet. Withdrawal availability can vary by asset, network, account, platform policy and current operational status.

Before buying an asset with the intention of moving it elsewhere, check the current withdrawal screen. Do not rely on an old review or forum post.

Custody

If the crypto remains inside the CoinSwitch account, the platform manages the custody infrastructure. This differs from self-custody, where you control the wallet keys.

Custodial platforms can reduce the complexity of managing seed phrases, but they also introduce platform and account-access risk.

Fees and spread

When comparing CoinSwitch with another exchange, review the trading fee, buy/sell spread, INR withdrawal cost, crypto withdrawal fee and any conversion cost.

CoinSwitch’s current Terms allow a separate service fee for its Assured Quantity Service on certain instant orders, plus transaction fees on some fiat or crypto transfers and withdrawals. A contract note is issued on each completed trade, recording the total price and any service fee or tax invoice. (CoinSwitch) So the real cost is more than a headline trading fee.

A platform that looks cheap based on one fee may not be cheapest for the full transaction route.

Tax records

Useful platform records can include transaction history, buy/sell data, TDS information where relevant, realised trade records and downloadable statements.

Keep your own copy of important records rather than relying on permanent access to an app account.

CoinSwitch vs CoinDCX vs Binance

A useful comparison should not ask “which exchange is best?”. Instead, ask:

  • Which has the most convenient INR route?
  • Which allows the withdrawals I need?
  • Which gives clearer tax records?
  • Which has the products I need?
  • Which total cost is lower for my use case?

Frequently asked questions

Its interface may be easier for some users, but platform suitability depends on fees, withdrawals, custody and the user's goals.

Can I withdraw crypto from CoinSwitch?

This can depend on the asset, network and current platform policy. Verify before buying.
