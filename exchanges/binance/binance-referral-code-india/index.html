Binance Referral Code L2DDNYDK: Sign-Up Bonus & Fee Discount for India

By Arjun Mehta · Research Editor · How we review

Last updated: 26 September 2026 · Sources checked: Binance Referral Pro documentation, FIU-IND registration records and Binance India announcements

Search for a Binance referral code and most results lead with the same promise: a big, fixed cash bonus. For a user signing up from India, that framing skips the part that actually matters.

The code L2DDNYDK is the referral identifier associated with CoinRaahi’s Binance invitation route. Entering it at sign-up creates a referral relationship, and the useful, predictable part of that relationship is a trading-fee discount.

Any separate cash bonus is tied to an active Binance campaign, which can start, end and change. So the honest summary is: the fee discount is the steady benefit; the sign-up bonus is the part that varies.

What a Binance referral code does and does not give you

Two of those three rows are the reason this guide exists. The fee discount is the part you keep for as long as you trade; the cash bonus is the part that changes with the campaign calendar.

What L2DDNYDK actually gives you

A referral code is often marketed as though the letters themselves produce a fixed reward. That is not how Binance’s system works. Three things should be kept separate:

  • Referral relationship — links your account to a referrer.
  • Referrer commission — Binance may pay the referrer a share of eligible trading fees you generate.
  • Your rebate or reward — depends on the referral configuration and any active campaign.

L2DDNYDK is the referral identifier for the invitation route in this guide. When you register with it, look at what Binance shows on the sign-up screen — the fee rebate, and any bonus tied to an active campaign. That screen is more reliable than a screenshot in an old article or a third-party promise.

The referral code and the referral link do the same job: the code is the identifier, and the link carries it. Use whichever Binance presents to you at sign-up.

KYC in India: the referral code is not identity verification

Using L2DDNYDK does not replace or reduce Binance’s KYC. Binance is registered with India’s Financial Intelligence Unit (FIU-IND) as a reporting entity under the anti-money-laundering framework. That status relates to reporting and AML/PMLA obligations — it is not a general financial-services licence, and it does not mean deposits are insured.

Indian KYC on Binance typically involves PAN, identity verification and, where asked, additional steps. A referral code and KYC serve two different purposes:

  • Referral: sets the referral relationship and the applicable commission or rebate arrangement.
  • KYC: verifies who you are and supports Binance’s compliance obligations.

Always follow the verification steps shown for your own account. Our KYC and account guide walks through the PAN and selfie steps in more detail.

INR funding: Binance uses P2P, not direct bank deposits

One point that many “sign-up bonus” articles get wrong: Binance does not offer direct INR bank deposits or a native UPI on-ramp the way Indian-first exchanges do. INR funding flows through the P2P marketplace.

After you create and verify the account, the practical INR route is P2P — you transfer INR to a seller and receive crypto. This adds counterparty checks that a direct bank deposit would not, so the price you see and the seller’s completion record matter. The Binance India guide and the how to buy USDT in India guide cover that route in full.

Tax on referral rewards in India

Referral rewards are not automatically tax-free. What you receive, why you received it and the nature of the transaction all matter.

India taxes gains from transferring virtual digital assets at 30% under Section 115BBH (plus applicable surcharge and cess), and 1% TDS applies to qualifying transfers under Section 393 of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (formerly Section 194S). A trading-fee rebate, a promotional reward, affiliate income and crypto received for a service are not necessarily the same thing for tax purposes.

In P2P, the responsibility for 1% TDS generally sits with the buyer or deductor under the rules rather than the platform — our 1% TDS guide explains who deducts in each scenario. For anything material, keep records of the asset, its value when received, the date and the reason, then check the treatment for your circumstances in the India crypto tax guide.

Bottom line

What does Binance referral code L2DDNYDK give an Indian user?

A referral relationship and, in most cases, a trading-fee discount whose percentage is set by the referrer and shown on your sign-up screen. It does not, by itself, guarantee a fixed cash bonus — that part depends on whichever Binance campaign is active.

If you are signing up for the first time, this is the moment to get the code right, because the referral relationship cannot normally be changed afterwards.
