Binance Guides for India

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Binance users in India often need answers to more than one question. Can you complete KYC? Which INR routes are currently available? Can you withdraw crypto to an external wallet? What fees apply? How should you keep records for tax purposes? This hub brings CoinRaahi’s Binance guides together in one place, with a focus on the practical details that matter to users in India.

Platform features, payment options and product availability can change, so time-sensitive information should always be checked against current Binance documentation before you act.

KYC and account setup

Identity verification is an important part of using major crypto platforms. CoinRaahi’s Binance coverage explains the types of identity checks users may encounter, why PAN and other information may be relevant, what to review if KYC is delayed, and account-security steps to complete after verification.

INR access and funding

Funding is one of the areas that can change most frequently. Indian users should check which INR-related routes are currently displayed, whether P2P or other payment options are available, whether crypto deposits are supported, and any limits, fees or verification requirements. Avoid assuming that an INR method shown today will remain unchanged.

For the step-by-step routes, see our guide on how to buy USDT in India with INR.

Trading fees and total cost

A low advertised trading fee does not always mean a low total cost. Your real cost can include maker or taker fees, conversion spreads, withdrawal fees, network charges, payment-route costs and price differences between markets. When comparing exchanges, CoinRaahi looks at the full route rather than one fee in isolation.

Crypto withdrawals and transfers

Before withdrawing crypto from Binance, check the asset, the network, destination address, memo or tag where required, minimum withdrawal, platform withdrawal fee, and whether the receiving wallet supports the same network. A successful trade and a successful blockchain transfer are two different steps.

Tax and record-keeping

Crypto tax rules in India are separate from exchange fees. Keep records of purchase date, purchase value, sale or transfer date, sale value, fees, TDS where applicable, transaction IDs and exchange statements. Binance vs Indian exchanges

Binance may differ from domestic exchanges in INR convenience, crypto withdrawals, supported assets, trading tools, tax reporting features and customer-support workflow. Rather than choosing a platform only by brand or advertised fee, compare the features that matter to your actual use case.

Binance safety checklist

  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Secure the email account connected to Binance.
  • Verify the website or app source before logging in.
  • Never share OTPs or passwords.
  • Check withdrawal addresses carefully.
  • Be cautious of fake support accounts.
  • Keep your own transaction records.

Frequently asked questions

Is Binance available to users in India?

Availability, KYC and product access should be checked separately. Platform features can vary and change over time.

Does Binance require KYC?

Major platform functions generally require identity verification. Check current Binance requirements before using the account.

Can users in India withdraw crypto from Binance?

Crypto withdrawal availability depends on the asset, network and account status. Check the withdrawal screen before buying an asset specifically for transfer.

Is Binance cheaper than Indian exchanges?

Not necessarily. Compare total cost, including spread, withdrawal cost and funding route, not only the headline trading fee.
