Compare Crypto Exchanges in India

Last updated: 6 September 2026

There is no single crypto exchange that is automatically right for every user in India. A platform that is convenient for INR deposits may offer fewer withdrawal options. Another may have more trading markets but require a different funding route. Tax records, custody, support and product access can also differ. CoinRaahi comparisons focus on these practical differences instead of declaring one platform “best” for everyone.

Do not compare only trading fees

A platform advertising 0.1% trading fees can still be more expensive overall if the spread is wider, withdrawal fees are higher, the funding route costs more, or INR conversion is less efficient. CoinRaahi uses a total-cost approach wherever possible.

INR access matters

Indian users should check UPI availability, bank transfer support, minimum deposit, withdrawal route, payment limits and processing times. Payment routes can change, so platform-specific articles should show when the information was last checked.

Crypto withdrawals matter too

A common mistake is choosing an exchange only for INR convenience. If you plan to move crypto to a wallet, also check whether withdrawals are enabled, which coins can be withdrawn, supported networks, minimum withdrawal amounts and fees.

Custody and control

Crypto held on an exchange is normally controlled through the exchange’s custody system. A self-custody wallet gives the user control of the private keys but also shifts responsibility to the user. Neither route is automatically right for everyone.

Tax handling and records

Platforms can differ in transaction reports, TDS handling, downloadable statements, trade history and tax summaries. These differences matter for record-keeping in India. See Crypto Tax in India for the wider rules.

Frequently asked questions

Which is the best crypto exchange in India?

There is no universal answer. It depends on INR access, withdrawals, fees, custody and the products you need.

Is the lowest-fee exchange always cheapest?

No. Spread, withdrawal fees and funding costs can matter just as much.

Should beginners use an Indian exchange?

Domestic platforms can be convenient for INR access, but users should still compare custody, withdrawals and fees.

Can I use more than one exchange?

Yes, some users use different platforms for INR access, trading or withdrawals. This can add complexity, so records should be kept carefully.
