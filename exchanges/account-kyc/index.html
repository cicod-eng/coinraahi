Crypto Exchange KYC & Account Problems in India

Last updated: 6 September 2026

KYC is one of the first points where new crypto users in India can run into problems.

A document may be valid but still fail because the account name does not match. A bank account may belong to the user but appear under a slightly different name. Facial verification may fail because of poor lighting or an outdated document.

The fastest way to solve KYC problems is to identify which part of the identity chain is inconsistent.

What does KYC actually check?

Depending on the platform, KYC can involve identity details, PAN, date of birth, address, government-issued documents, facial verification and bank or payment account ownership. Not every platform uses the same workflow.

The identity chain

Before submitting documents, make sure the following details are consistent:

  • Exchange account name
  • PAN / identity record
  • Bank account holder name
  • Date of birth
  • Mobile / email details where relevant

Small spelling differences can create unnecessary verification problems.

PAN and crypto accounts

PAN is important to many financial and tax-related workflows in India. When a crypto platform asks for PAN information, make sure the number is correct, the name matches, the date of birth is consistent, and you are not using another person’s PAN.

Do not attempt to bypass a KYC mismatch using someone else’s documents.

Why bank name matching matters

A payment may come from a bank account whose name is formatted differently from the exchange account. Examples include a middle name omitted, surname order changed, initials used, or a spelling variation.

If a platform requires the payment method to belong to the verified user, this mismatch can trigger delays or rejection.

KYC pending

A pending status does not always mean the process failed. Possible reasons include manual review, document quality, additional compliance checks, high verification demand or incomplete information.

Check the account message before resubmitting. Submitting the same incorrect information repeatedly can delay resolution.

KYC rejected

If verification is rejected:

  1. Read the rejection reason.
  2. Confirm the document type is accepted.
  3. Check expiry and readability.
  4. Match the account information exactly.
  5. Retake facial verification if requested.
  6. Contact official support if the reason is unclear.

Never send identity documents to Telegram, WhatsApp or unofficial support accounts.

Account restrictions

Account restrictions can arise for reasons unrelated to basic KYC. These can include a security review, unusual login activity, payment disputes, compliance review, incomplete verification or withdrawal protection measures.

Do not assume every restricted account has the same cause.

Security after KYC

Once verification is complete:

  • Enable 2FA.
  • Secure the connected email account.
  • Add a passkey if supported.
  • Review logged-in devices.
  • Enable anti-phishing tools if available.
  • Use withdrawal protection features.

KYC protects platform compliance. It does not replace account security.

Never share these with support

A legitimate support process should not require you to give someone your password, OTP, seed phrase, wallet private key or remote control of your device.

Identity-document handling should occur only through the official platform process.

Frequently asked questions

Do crypto exchanges in India require KYC?

Major platforms generally require identity verification for core account functions.

Why does my PAN name not match my exchange account?

Differences in initials, surname order or spelling can create a mismatch.

Can I use someone else's bank account?

Platforms may require payments to come from an account belonging to the verified user. Check the platform rules.

Why is my KYC pending?

It may require manual or additional review.

Should I send documents to support on WhatsApp?

No. Use only official platform channels.
