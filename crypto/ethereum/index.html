Ethereum Explained for India

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Ethereum and ETH are related, but they are not the same thing. Ethereum is the blockchain network. ETH is the network’s native asset. That simple distinction prevents a lot of beginner confusion.

What makes Ethereum different from Bitcoin?

Bitcoin focuses strongly on a decentralised monetary asset and settlement network. Ethereum was designed as a programmable blockchain where developers can deploy smart contracts. That enables tokens, stablecoins, DeFi, NFTs and RWA applications.

What is gas?

Ethereum transactions require computational resources. Users pay network fees in ETH. Gas is not an exchange fee. It is part of how Ethereum prices network execution.

Why can gas become expensive?

Costs can rise when network demand increases, complex smart contracts require more computation, or users compete for block space. This is one reason Layer 2 networks became important.

What are Layer 2 networks?

Layer 2 systems process activity outside or alongside Ethereum’s main execution layer while using Ethereum for parts of settlement/security. Beginners should still check which network they are on, bridge requirements, supported wallet and withdrawal path.

Ethereum tokens

Many assets use Ethereum token standards. Examples include stablecoins, governance tokens and tokenised assets. Holding a token on Ethereum does not mean holding ETH itself.

Smart contracts introduce new risks

A smart contract can automate rules. It can also contain bugs, design flaws, permission risks and upgrade controls. “Decentralised” does not automatically mean “risk-free”.

What is Ethereum staking?

Ethereum uses proof of stake. Users can participate directly or through services, but the risk differs depending on whether the route uses direct validator staking, exchange staking or liquid staking tokens. Each adds different custody and smart-contract layers.

Ethereum wallet safety

Before using Ethereum, keep ETH for gas, verify token contracts, review approvals, avoid blind signing, and distinguish mainnet from Layer 2 networks.

Frequently asked questions

Is ETH a company share?

No. ETH is the native asset of the Ethereum network.

Why do I need ETH to send USDT on Ethereum?

Because Ethereum network execution fees are normally paid in ETH.

Is Ethereum the same as ERC20?

No. Ethereum is the blockchain; ERC20 is a token standard used on Ethereum.

Can Ethereum transactions be reversed?

Normally not after final confirmation.
