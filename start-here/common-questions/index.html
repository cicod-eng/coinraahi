Common Crypto Questions in India

Last updated: 6 September 2026

This page is designed for questions that are too important for a one-line answer but do not always need a 3,000-word guide.

Is crypto legal in India?

Avoid reducing this to a simple “yes” or “no”. Crypto-assets are used and traded in India within an environment that includes VDA taxation, KYC/reporting obligations and platform-level compliance requirements. That does not make crypto legal tender, and it does not mean every product or platform has the same regulatory status.

Is Bitcoin legal tender in India?

No. Bitcoin is not the official currency of India. The existence of VDA tax rules should not be confused with legal-tender status.

Why is crypto taxed at 30%?

India applies a special tax regime to qualifying income from transfers of virtual digital assets under Section 115BBH. See the dedicated tax guide for current details and exceptions.

What is 1% TDS on crypto?

Section 194S can apply 1% TDS to qualifying VDA transfer consideration under applicable conditions and thresholds. It is a withholding mechanism, not the same thing as a 1% exchange fee.

Do I need PAN to use crypto exchanges?

India-focused exchange workflows commonly use PAN and identity information as part of KYC and tax/compliance processes. Requirements vary by platform.

Is USDT the same as a US dollar?

No. USDT is a token issued within Tether's stablecoin framework and designed to track USD value. It is not your personal US bank deposit.

Can I lose crypto by choosing the wrong network?

Yes. The blockchain transaction can succeed while the receiving platform fails to credit it if the network is unsupported.

Can crypto transactions be reversed?

A confirmed blockchain transfer generally cannot be reversed like a card payment. Recovery depends on the specific mistake.

Is a wallet safer than an exchange?

Not automatically. A wallet removes some exchange-custody risk but adds private-key, recovery and transaction risks.

Should I keep all my crypto in one place?

Not necessarily. But splitting funds across multiple platforms and wallets also increases operational complexity.

What is a seed phrase?

A recovery credential used by many self-custody wallets. Anyone who obtains it may be able to recreate the wallet.

Should I share my seed phrase with support?

No. Legitimate support should never need your seed phrase or private key.

Is P2P cheaper than an exchange?

Sometimes, sometimes not. Compare the INR-per-crypto quote, counterparty risk and transaction structure rather than assuming “no platform fee” means cheaper.

Are tokenised stocks real stocks?

Sometimes they may represent or be backed by real securities, but the holder's legal rights depend on the product structure.

Is digital gold the same as a Gold ETF?

No. They can differ in legal structure, custody, regulation, liquidity and redemption.
