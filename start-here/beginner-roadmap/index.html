A Beginner Roadmap to Crypto

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Most crypto mistakes happen because users learn in the wrong order. They learn trading before custody, yield before wallet recovery, and “which coin to buy” before understanding what a blockchain transaction is. This roadmap fixes that order.

Stage 1 — Understand what you are looking at

Learn the difference between Bitcoin, Ethereum, stablecoins, tokens and traditional securities. Do not begin with price predictions. Begin with: what is this product?

Stage 2 — Understand exchanges

Before opening accounts everywhere, learn spot trading, market vs limit orders, spread, trading fees, INR access, KYC and crypto withdrawals. An exchange should be evaluated by what you need it to do, not by brand recognition alone.

Stage 3 — Understand custody

Learn exchange custody, self-custody, seed phrase, private key, recovery and hardware wallets. Do not move a large amount to a wallet until you understand how you would recover it if your phone disappeared.

Stage 4 — Learn one transfer properly

Understand deposit address, network, withdrawal fee, memo/tag, TXID and blockchain explorer. One carefully understood transfer teaches more than watching ten “how to send crypto” videos.

Stage 5 — Build security habits

Before increasing your balance, enable strong 2FA or passkeys, secure your email, avoid screen-sharing with strangers, verify support channels, learn common phishing patterns, and protect recovery phrases offline.

Stage 6 — Understand India-specific rules

Learn the difference between exchange fees, TDS, VDA tax and transaction records. Do not treat all of them as one “30% crypto tax”.

Stage 7 — Only then explore advanced products

After the foundations, you can study staking, DeFi, tokenised stocks, tokenised gold, RWA, lending and derivatives. Advanced products often add more layers of issuer, smart-contract or liquidation risk.

What not to do in your first week

  • Leverage
  • Copying Telegram traders
  • Unknown wallet extensions
  • Chasing high APY
  • Sending large test-free transfers
  • Buying something because it is “trending”

A 30-day learning plan

Week 1: Crypto basics
Week 2: Exchanges, fees and KYC
Week 3: Wallets and transfers
Week 4: Security, tax basics and asset comparison

The goal is not to become a trader in 30 days. The goal is to stop being easy to mislead.

Frequently asked questions

What should a crypto beginner learn first?

Start with the asset, then exchanges, custody, transfers, security and local tax basics.

Should I start with Bitcoin?

Bitcoin is a useful starting point because it introduces blockchain ownership without many extra product layers.

When should I learn DeFi?

After you understand wallets, gas, token approvals and transaction signing.

Do beginners need a hardware wallet?

Not automatically. The need depends on balance size, holding period and your ability to manage recovery safely.
