About Arjun

Arjun is a Research Editor at CoinRaahi, where he covers cryptocurrency exchanges, USDT, wallets, transfers, crypto tax and digital assets for Indian readers. His approach is practical rather than promotional: articles are built on official documentation, current platform rules, and hands-on testing where possible. Because platforms, fees, tax rules and regulation can change, guides are reviewed and updated when important information changes. Arjun does not provide personalised investment, legal or tax advice.

  • Stablecoins and tokenised assets
  • Security and scam prevention

How I research articles

Guides are researched from primary sources wherever possible — official exchange documentation and fee schedules, FIU-IND notices, Income Tax Department resources, Indian legislation, RBI and SEBI publications where relevant, blockchain explorers, official wallet documentation, and first-hand platform testing. When a fact can change quickly — fees, payment methods, KYC or withdrawal rules — it is checked again during updates. Where something cannot be independently confirmed, the article says so rather than presenting an assumption as fact.

Hands-on testing

Some guides include first-hand testing of registration flows, KYC screens, INR purchase routes, P2P processes, deposits and withdrawals, wallet transfers, fee comparisons and network availability. When a guide includes hands-on testing, we aim to state when the test was performed and distinguish observed results from general platform rules. Screens, fees and features can differ by account, location and date.

Sources I use

  • Income Tax Department
  • FIU-IND
  • Reserve Bank of India
  • Securities and Exchange Board of India
  • Official exchange help centres and fee schedules
  • Blockchain documentation and explorers
