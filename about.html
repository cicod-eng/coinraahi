About CoinRaahi

What CoinRaahi is

CoinRaahi is a practical guide to crypto and global digital assets for India. "Raahi" means traveller in Hindi — because we help you navigate from your first rupee into crypto, US stocks, gold, and tokenised assets.

Why we're different

Most crypto content online comes from platforms that want you to sign up for their product. That shapes what they tell you. We don't run an exchange, so we can explain the differences platforms would rather not talk about — fees, TDS handling, INR deposit routes, and what you actually own.

What we cover

  • Exchanges — Binance, CoinDCX, CoinSwitch, comparisons, KYC, and fees.
  • Crypto — Bitcoin, Ethereum, stablecoins, and blockchain basics.
  • Assets & RWA — US stocks and ETFs, tokenised stocks, gold, and real-world assets.
  • Wallets & Safety — wallet basics, transfers, account security, and scam prevention.
