Editorial Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

What CoinRaahi publishes

CoinRaahi publishes educational guides on cryptocurrency, US stocks and ETFs, gold, and tokenised assets — written for readers in India. We explain how these products work, what they cost, how you can access them, and what you actually own. We do not give buy or sell recommendations.

How we research

We base our guides on official sources — including the Income Tax Department, FIU-IND, RBI and SEBI — and on each platform's own documentation. Where a fact can change quickly (fees, INR deposit routes, supported assets, TDS handling), we say when it was checked and encourage you to verify the current details with the provider.

Our principles

  • Accuracy first. We would rather leave out a number than state one we cannot verify.
  • Independence. We do not run an exchange or brokerage, and we do not let relationships shape what we explain. See our Affiliate Disclosure.
  • Risk disclosure. We state the risks, costs and tax implications of a product, not just its benefits.
  • Plain English. We write for beginners and avoid jargon where a simpler word works.

Corrections

If you spot an error or something out of date, email hello@coinraahi.com. We correct factual errors promptly and note significant updates in the guide itself.

What we are not

CoinRaahi is not a licensed financial adviser, broker, or tax adviser. Our content is for information and education only and is not financial, investment, legal or tax advice. See our Risk Disclosure.
