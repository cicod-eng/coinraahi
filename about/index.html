About CoinRaahi

Last updated: 21 September 2026

CoinRaahi is an independent educational website that helps readers in India understand cryptocurrency, exchanges, stablecoins, wallets, crypto tax, US stocks, gold and tokenised assets. We explain what these things cost, how to access them, what you actually own, and where they can go wrong — without hiding the risks, costs or regulatory uncertainty.

"Raahi" means traveller in Hindi. We help you navigate from your first rupee into crypto and global digital assets.

Who CoinRaahi is for

CoinRaahi is written for readers in India who want a clear, practical explanation before they act. That includes people buying crypto for the first time, comparing Binance, CoinDCX or CoinSwitch, moving assets between an exchange and a wallet, checking fees before a transaction, working out TDS and tax, and avoiding scams or transfer mistakes.

What we cover

  • Exchanges — Binance, CoinDCX, CoinSwitch and WazirX, plus comparisons, KYC, INR access, P2P, deposits, withdrawals and fees.
  • Stablecoins and crypto — USDT, Bitcoin, Ethereum, blockchain networks and transfer costs.
  • Wallets and safety — self-custody, transfers, network selection, seed phrase security and common scams.
  • Crypto tax in India — VDA tax, TDS, gains and losses, and how tax applies to everyday transactions.
  • US stocks, gold and RWA — US stocks, gold-backed assets, tokenised securities and real-world assets, including custody and ownership differences.

Our approach

  • Practical first. We start from the reader's actual question and answer it before adding background.
  • Primary sources where possible. We prefer the Income Tax Department, FIU-IND, RBI, SEBI, and the platforms' own documentation over second-hand summaries.
  • Risks stay visible. Tax, withdrawal risk, custody risk, regulatory uncertainty and hidden costs are explained where they matter, not buried at the bottom.

How we research and review

Every guide goes through a structured process — defining the question, checking primary sources and India-specific conditions, verifying fees, testing where practical, and separating facts from estimates. The full process is on our How We Review page.

Hands-on testing

Where practical, we test part of a process first-hand — registration, KYC, P2P flows, INR options, wallet transfers and fee displays. When a guide includes testing, we state when it was done and make clear the result was observed during that test. Interfaces and features change and may differ by account.

Who writes CoinRaahi

CoinRaahi content is written and reviewed by editors who focus on practical crypto and digital-asset topics for Indian readers. Author profiles explain what each editor covers, how they research, which sources they use and whether they perform hands-on testing. You can reach an article's author profile to learn who is responsible for that content.

Editorial independence

CoinRaahi may earn money through affiliate partnerships, but a commercial relationship never determines whether risks are mentioned, whether fees are shown, or how claims are checked. We do not present a paid relationship as an independent factual conclusion. See our Affiliate Disclosure.

Accuracy and updates

Crypto platforms and rules change quickly — fees, KYC, INR payment options, supported networks, tax guidance and regulatory status. Important guides show a publication or review date, and when something changes materially we update the article rather than leave it outdated.

Corrections

If you spot an error or something out of date, tell us and we will review it against evidence. See our Corrections Policy.

What CoinRaahi is not

CoinRaahi is an educational publication. We do not give personalised investment, financial, legal or tax advice, and nothing here is a recommendation to buy, sell or hold a particular asset. Verify important information before acting.
