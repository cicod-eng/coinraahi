Corrections Policy

Last updated: 21 September 2026

CoinRaahi aims to publish accurate, practical and current information for readers in India. Because we cover exchanges, wallets, taxes, fees, regulation and digital assets, some information changes quickly. When we find a material error or an outdated claim, we correct it as soon as reasonably possible.

What we correct

We update or correct an article when we identify issues such as incorrect fees, outdated withdrawal limits, changed KYC requirements, inaccurate tax information, outdated regulatory status, unavailable payment methods, unsupported networks, or misleading wording that could affect a reader's understanding. Minor grammar and formatting changes are made without a formal note.

Material corrections

A material correction is a change that could meaningfully affect how a reader understands or acts. Examples include correcting a tax rate, a withdrawal fee or a platform's regulatory status, or fixing a transfer instruction that could cause a loss of funds. When a material change is made, we may update the article's review date and, where appropriate, add a short note explaining what changed.

How we verify corrections

Before a significant correction, we try to confirm the new information against reliable sources — the Income Tax Department, FIU-IND, RBI, SEBI, official exchange documentation and fee schedules, wallet documentation, blockchain explorers, or first-hand testing. We prefer primary sources whenever they are available.

Reader-submitted corrections

If you believe an article contains an error, contact us. It helps to include the article URL, the statement that may be wrong, an explanation, and a supporting source or screenshot where possible. We review correction requests against evidence rather than preference or commercial interest.

Platform changes

Exchanges change fees, networks, KYC, INR access and product availability, sometimes without notice. When an article was accurate at review but later changed, we treat that as an update rather than an error.

Tax and regulatory updates

Tax and regulatory content gets extra care because outdated information can materially affect users. When laws or official guidance change, we review the relevant articles and update them. CoinRaahi does not provide personalised tax or legal advice.

Affiliate relationships and corrections

Affiliate relationships do not prevent us from correcting negative or unfavourable information. If an affiliate partner changes its fees, restrictions or status, we update the article the same way we would for a non-affiliate platform. Corrections are based on evidence, not commercial relationships.

Historical accuracy

Where useful we keep older facts for context — for example, that a feature was available before a date but is no longer supported — and make the timing clear so readers do not confuse history with current conditions.

Contact

If you believe something should be corrected or reviewed, reach us through the Contact page and include the article URL and a supporting source where possible.
