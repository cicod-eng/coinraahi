USDT to INR Calculator

Last updated: 22 September 2026

Convert USDT to INR, or INR to USDT, using the rate your exchange actually quotes. The result is the approximate value before any fee, spread or TDS.

₹0.00

Which rate should I enter?

Use the price your exchange or P2P listing shows right now — not a market tracker alone. In India, USDT often trades a few percent above the official USD/INR rate because of local demand. Entering the official rate will understate what you actually pay. See our USDT premium data for the current gap.

What this does not include

  • Exchange or P2P fees and the buy/sell spread.
  • Withdrawal fees if you later move the USDT out.
  • The 1% TDS that may apply to certain transfers under Section 194S.

Frequently asked questions

Is 1 USDT always worth ₹1 × the dollar rate?

No. USDT aims to track USD, but its rupee price on Indian exchanges depends on local demand and can sit above the official USD/INR rate.

Does this calculator include fees?

No. Enter your exchange's effective quote and treat any fee or spread separately.
