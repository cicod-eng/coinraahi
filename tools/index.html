Crypto Tools for India

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Two quick calculators for Indian crypto users: a USDT-to-INR converter and a simple capital-gains tax estimator based on Section 115BBH. These are estimates, not tax advice.

USDT to INR converter

Enter the USDT amount and the current USDT price in rupees shown by your exchange (not a market tracker alone). The result is the approximate INR value before any fee, spread or TDS.

India crypto tax estimator

Under Section 115BBH, a gain on virtual digital assets is generally taxed at 30% plus applicable cess, and only the cost of acquisition is deductible — losses cannot generally be set off. This tool applies 30% + 4% cess (an effective 31.2%) to your sale value minus cost, for a rough figure.

What these estimates leave out

  • The 1% TDS already deducted is typically adjusted against your final tax, not added on top.
  • Your actual tax can differ based on your slab, cess and any specific rules or thresholds.
  • Recovery tokens, airdrops and certain transfers can have their own treatment.

Use these numbers only as a starting point and confirm with a qualified professional. See our 30% crypto tax guide.

Frequently asked questions

Is 31.2% the exact crypto tax in India?

No. 30% plus 4% cess is a common shorthand; your actual liability can differ based on your situation. Treat 31.2% as an estimate.

Does the USDT to INR converter include fees?

No. Enter the effective rate your platform quotes and consider any spread or fee separately.

Is this tax advice?

No. This is an educational estimate only. Confirm with a qualified professional.
