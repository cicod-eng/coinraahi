Withdrawal Pending? 3-Step Checker

Last updated: 22 September 2026

A withdrawal that looks stuck is almost always stuck in one of three places. Answer two short questions to find out which one — and what to do next.

Step 1 — Do you have a transaction hash (TXID) for this withdrawal?

Look in the withdrawal history of the sending platform. A TXID is a long string of letters and numbers that appears once the platform has broadcast the transaction.

Step 2 — Is the TXID confirmed on a block explorer?

Paste the TXID into a block explorer for the network you used (e.g. Etherscan for ERC-20, Tronscan for TRC-20, a Bitcoin explorer for BTC).

Platform stage — the withdrawal has not been sent to the blockchain.

If there is no TXID, the transaction is still inside the sending platform (awaiting approval, security review, or processing). This is the most common case and usually resolves on its own within the platform's stated time.

  • Check for an approval or verification step (email/SMS/2FA) you may have missed.
  • Do not send the same withdrawal again immediately — wait for the stated processing window.
  • If it stays stuck past the platform's stated time, contact support and quote the withdrawal ID (not a TXID, since none exists yet).

Blockchain stage — broadcast but not yet confirmed.

The transaction is on the network, waiting to be included in a block. Confirmation time depends on the network and the fee set by the sender, not on the exchange.

  • Note the network — a TRC-20 transfer and an ERC-20 transfer can take very different times.
  • Do not send again: a pending transaction will still go through once confirmed.
  • Watch the explorer for the first confirmation, then re-check the destination.

Sent on the wrong network? guide

Receiving-platform stage — confirmed on-chain but not credited.

The funds have left the sender and are confirmed on the blockchain, but the receiving platform or wallet has not credited them. This is now a receiving-side issue, not a network issue.

  • Confirm the address and network match what the receiving side expects.
  • Check the receiving side's minimum deposit and any required memo/tag.
  • Contact the receiving platform's support with: TXID, network, amount, receiving address, and the time of the transfer.
