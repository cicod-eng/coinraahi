Crypto Tax Calculator (India)

Last updated: 22 September 2026

A rough capital-gains estimate under Section 115BBH: 30% tax plus 4% cess (an effective 31.2%), with your already-deducted 1% TDS adjusted against the final tax. Estimates only — not tax advice.

Capital gain: ₹0

Tax (30% + 4% cess): ₹0

Less TDS already deducted: ₹0

Net tax payable: ₹0

How this is calculated

  • Gain = sale value − cost of acquisition (only the cost of acquisition is generally deductible).
  • Tax = gain × 30%, plus 4% cess on that tax → an effective 31.2% on the gain.
  • Your 1% TDS (Section 194S) is adjusted against the final tax, not added on top. Net payable = tax − TDS already deducted (never below zero).
  • Losses on one asset generally cannot be set off against gains on another.

What this estimate leaves out

  • Your actual liability can differ based on your slab, cess and any specific rules or thresholds.
  • Recovery tokens, airdrops, certain transfers, staking and DeFi activity can have their own treatment.
  • This uses a single round-trip (one sale minus one cost). Multiple lots and assets need a proper record-keeping review.

Confirm with a qualified professional. Read our crypto tax in India guide for the full rules.

Frequently asked questions

Is 31.2% the exact crypto tax in India?

No. 30% plus 4% cess is a common shorthand; your actual liability can differ based on your situation. Treat 31.2% as an estimate.

Do I pay 30% and 1% TDS on top of each other?

Generally not. The 1% TDS already deducted is adjusted against your final tax, so it is not an additional tax on top of the 30%.

Can I offset a loss on one crypto against a gain on another?

Under Section 115BBH, losses from virtual digital assets generally cannot be set off against other gains.

Is this tax advice?

No. This is an educational estimate only. Confirm with a qualified professional.
